# Brag Plan: SamadhanSetu

## What is this app?
SamadhanSetu is Jharkhand's AI-powered civic challenge resolution platform that triages citizen grievances in under 15 milliseconds and bridges recurring municipal failures directly to universities and industry partners for permanent engineering solutions.

## The angle
Sovereign civic intelligence. Instead of presenting as a routine municipal complaint box, SamadhanSetu is framed as a high-tech state operating system that eliminates clerical backlogs with deterministic AI and transforms civic complaints into applied R&D capstones at premier engineering institutions (BIT Mesra, NIT Jamshedpur, IIT ISM Dhanbad).

## Hook (first 2-3 seconds)
An authoritative, glowing sovereign emblem of Jharkhand emerges from deep forest teal darkness with the quiet, provocative question: "When a million citizens voice a crisis, who listens?"

## Key moments (the middle)
- **15ms Deterministic AI Triage**: A real civic docket from Katras, Dhanbad animates with live GPS coordinates, spinning up an 88/100 Auto-Approved authenticity badge with verified multi-signal flags.
- **The Triple Helix Solver Exchange**: The docket transitions seamlessly into an Academic Challenge card adopted by IIT (ISM) Dhanbad and BIT Mesra, sponsored by industrial CSR funds.
- **24-District Telemetry & SLA Velocity**: Real-time state metrics highlighting a 92.4% resolution rate and 2.1-day average SLA.

## Outro / punchline
The official golden Setu seal locks into place with bilingual typography: "SAMADHAN SETU • समाधान सेतु — From Pothole Patching to State Innovation."

## User flow worth showing
Entry (Citizen files Katras road cave-in with GPS) → Action (Deterministic AI Pre-Screening validates 6 signals in 15ms) → Result (High-Confidence ticket auto-dispatched to department queue & adopted by university solver hub).
