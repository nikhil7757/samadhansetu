Top Recommendation

Tackle Candidate 1: Unify "Complaint" and "Problem" into GrievanceRegistry

The codebase currently splits a civic grievance across two disjoint models: a citizen's complaint (with public tracking ID and AI score) and a community problem (with university solver teams). Unifying them into a single deep GrievanceRegistry eliminates schema fragmentation, cuts out duplicate endpoints, and establishes a clean end-to-end pipeline from citizen filing to ground resolution.
